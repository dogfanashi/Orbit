Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit ("Orbit", "we", "us", or "our") may collect, process, use, retain, and protect information when you use the Orbit Discord bot, website, and related services.

By using Orbit, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled and used by a Discord server, Orbit may process certain information necessary to provide its functionality.

This may include:

  • Discord User IDs;
  • Discord Server IDs;
  • Discord Channel IDs;
  • Discord Role IDs;
  • Discord usernames or display names where necessary;
  • Server configuration settings;
  • Command usage information;
  • Moderation records;
  • Warnings, infractions, or other administrative records;
  • Ticket information;
  • Bot configuration data;
  • Economy or other feature-related data where applicable; and
  • Other information reasonably necessary to provide requested features.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Orbit does not intentionally request or require users to provide sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the operation of the service.

Orbit does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account authentication credentials, payment card numbers, or similar highly sensitive credentials through the Discord bot.

You should never provide passwords, authentication tokens, or other private credentials to Orbit or anyone claiming to represent Orbit.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by Orbit may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Orbit's features;
  • Maintain server configurations;
  • Perform moderation and administrative functions requested by server administrators;
  • Maintain logging and security features;
  • Provide economy or other optional features;
  • Troubleshoot technical problems;
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access;
  • Maintain and improve service reliability;
  • Respond to support requests; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Data Minimization

Orbit is designed to collect and process information reasonably necessary for the features being used.

Orbit does not intentionally collect personal information solely for the purpose of collecting it.

The information actually collected and retained by Orbit depends on the features implemented and enabled in the version of Orbit being operated.

6. Discord Information

Orbit may receive information through Discord's APIs when Orbit is installed in a server or used by a Discord user.

Orbit's ability to access Discord information depends on the permissions granted to the bot and the functionality provided by Discord.

Orbit does not control Discord's independent collection or processing of information. Discord's own privacy practices are governed by Discord's applicable policies.

7. Administrator-Controlled Information

Server administrators may configure Orbit to perform moderation, logging, ticketing, security, or other administrative functions.

Information generated through these features may be visible to authorized server administrators or staff depending on the configuration of the Discord server.

Orbit is not responsible for how individual server administrators choose to use or disclose information obtained through their server.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

Orbit does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure, hosting, database, security, analytics, or other service providers when reasonably necessary to operate Orbit.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide the services they provide to Orbit, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Orbit may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law;
  • Respond to valid legal processes;
  • Protect Orbit, its users, or its infrastructure;
  • Investigate suspected abuse, fraud, or security incidents; or
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Orbit or others.

9. Data Retention

Orbit retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested features, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise operate the service.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature that generated it.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, Orbit may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable practices and legal requirements.

10. Data Deletion Requests

Users or authorized server administrators may contact the official Orbit support team regarding applicable data deletion requests.

Orbit may request sufficient information to verify the request and identify the relevant account, server, or information.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, necessary to resolve disputes, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

11. Security

Orbit uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-connected service, database, or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe that Orbit has experienced a security incident or that your information may have been compromised, please contact the official Orbit support team as soon as reasonably possible.

12. Children's Privacy

Orbit does not intentionally request or collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

If you believe that a child has provided information to Orbit in a manner that should not have occurred, please contact the official Orbit support team so the matter can be reviewed.

13. Cookies and Website Information

The Orbit website may use technically necessary technologies to provide website functionality, security, or performance.

If analytics, cookies, or other tracking technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to accurately describe their use.

14. Third-Party Services

Orbit may rely on third-party services, including Discord, hosting providers, database providers, payment processors, security providers, and other infrastructure providers.

Third-party services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Orbit is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

15. Data Transfers

Depending on the infrastructure used to operate Orbit, information may be processed or stored on systems located outside the user's state or country.

Where applicable, Orbit intends to use reasonable safeguards for information processed through its service providers.

16. Texas and United States Privacy Rights

Orbit intends to comply with applicable United States federal privacy requirements and applicable Texas privacy and data-protection laws.

Depending on applicable law and whether Orbit is subject to a particular statutory requirement, users may have certain rights regarding their personal data.

These rights may include rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information concerning the processing of applicable personal data, subject to applicable legal limitations and exceptions.

Requests may be submitted through the official Orbit support team.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Orbit may update this Privacy Policy when its features, data practices, legal obligations, or service providers change.

The "Last Updated" date will identify the current version of this policy.

Where appropriate, material changes may be communicated through the Orbit website, Discord server, or another reasonable method.

18. Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, security concerns, or other privacy matters should be directed to the official Orbit support team.

19. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of August 13, 2026.

By continuing to use Orbit after this Privacy Policy is updated, you acknowledge the updated policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.
