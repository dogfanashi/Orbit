Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

1. Information Orbit May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, Orbit may process Discord identifiers such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message or command information, configuration settings, moderation records, ticket information, and other data needed to operate requested features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot features, maintain server configuration, perform moderation and logging functions requested by administrators, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Data Minimization

Orbit should only collect and retain information reasonably necessary for its features. The actual data stored by Orbit should match the implementation deployed by the developers.

4. Sharing

Orbit does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers required to operate Orbit, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Retention and Deletion

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the feature using it. Server administrators may contact the Orbit support team regarding deletion requests where applicable.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Orbit's features and data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or requests should be directed to the official Orbit support team.
